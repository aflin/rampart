THIRD-PARTY FILES -- NOT PART OF RAMPART

The 52 .odt documents in this directory come from the test suite of pandoc
(https://github.com/jgm/pandoc).  They are used here as an independent corpus
for checking rampart-totext's output against a converter written by someone
else -- fixtures whose expected results rampart did not choose.

They are test DATA.  No pandoc code is compiled into, linked against, or
invoked by rampart.
